# Code of Conduct

ProxyBot is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior.
- Respect privacy, consent, and responsible security disclosure.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, reviews, project chat, and public events where someone is representing ProxyBot.
